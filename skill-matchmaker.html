    How these scores are calculated

    Match: 100 × Σ(min(current, required) × weight) ÷ Σ(required × weight).

    Gap index: 100 × Σ(max(required − current, 0) × weight) ÷ Σ(required × weight).

    Readiness: 0.8 × skill match + 0.2 × profile completeness (0% skill match + 0% profile completion). Scores are prototype indicators, not a hiring guarantee.

    Gap labels: Strong meets the requirement; Moderate is at least halfway there; Missing / Critical is below half. Critical gaps are prioritized before other gaps.
